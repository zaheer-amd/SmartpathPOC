# CSD-N Policy Guidelines (Non-Automated Extended Health Care)

## 1. Policy Overview
- **Policy Code:** CSD N
- **Classification:** Non-automated / Manual Adjudication
- **Coverage Category:** Extended Health Care (EHC) - Vision Care

## 2. Vision Benefit Rules & Coverage Limits
- **Eligible Items:** Prescription eyeglasses (lenses and frames) or contact lenses prescribed by an authorized ophthalmologist or optometrist.
- **Coverage Coinsurance:** 100% of eligible expenses up to the maximum benefit limit.
- **Benefit Maximum Limit:** $200.00 per covered individual.
- **Frequency Limit / Rolling Period:** Once every 24 consecutive months (24 MON).
- **Deductible:** $0.00 (No deductible applied to Vision benefits).

## 3. Adjudication & Calculation Rules
- **Total Claim / Receipt Amount:** The total cost billed on the official provider receipt.
- **Eligible Amount Calculation:**
  - If Receipt Total $\le$ Benefit Limit ($200.00): Eligible Amount = Receipt Total.
  - If Receipt Total $>$ Benefit Limit ($200.00): Eligible Amount = $200.00 (capped at policy maximum).
- **Out-of-Pocket (OOP) / Member Responsibility:**
  - $\text{OOP Amount} = \text{Receipt Total} - \text{Eligible Amount}$.
  - Any portion exceeding the $200.00 limit is non-reimbursable and becomes member out-of-pocket.
- **Adjudication Decisions:**
  - **Approve:** Selected when the claim is for a valid vision item and the eligible amount and member out-of-pocket amounts are correctly determined according to limits.
  - **Deny:** Selected only when the claimant has exceeded frequency limits (e.g., claimed within previous 24 months) or item is non-eligible under policy terms.
  - **Manual Review:** Selected if receipt is illegible, missing prescription details, or requires supervisor override.
