# SmartPath Policy Document: Pharmacy RX (Preferred Tier 1)
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Plan Type:** Prescription Drug Plan (Retail)

## Formulary Tiers and Copayments
1. **Tier 1 - Preferred Generics**
   - Includes widely used, low-cost generic medications (e.g., Amoxicillin, Lisinopril).
   - **Copayment:** Fixed $15.00 copay per 30-day supply.

2. **Tier 2 - Non-Preferred Generics & Preferred Brands**
   - **Copayment:** Fixed $45.00 copay per 30-day supply.

3. **Tier 3 - Non-Preferred Brands**
   - **Copayment:** Fixed $85.00 copay per 30-day supply.

## Adjudication Rules for Adjusters
- For retail pharmacy claims, the member is only responsible for the fixed copayment associated with the drug's tier.
- **Eligible Amount:** The total billed amount minus the member's fixed copayment.
- **Out-of-Pocket Amount (OOP):** The member's fixed copayment amount.
- If the total billed amount is LESS than the copayment, the member pays the billed amount (OOP = Billed Amount, Eligible = $0).
