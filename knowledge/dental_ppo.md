# SmartPath Policy Document: Dental PPO (Standard Coverage)
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Plan Type:** Preferred Provider Organization (PPO)

## Coverage Classes and Co-Insurance
1. **Class A - Preventative & Diagnostic (D0100 - D0999)**
   - Includes Comprehensive Oral Exams (D0150) and Intraoral X-Rays (D0210).
   - **Coverage:** 100% of billed charges (In-Network).
   - **Deductible:** Waived for Class A services.

2. **Class B - Basic Restorative (D2000 - D2999)**
   - Includes Amalgam and Composite Fillings (e.g., D2330).
   - **Coverage:** 80% of billed charges (In-Network) after deductible is met.
   - **Deductible:** Subject to the $50 individual annual deductible.

## General Limitations
- **Annual Maximum:** $1,500 per member per calendar year.

## Adjudication Rules for Adjusters
- First, check if the annual deductible has been met in the member's ledger.
- If the deductible is NOT met, subtract the remaining deductible amount from the eligible Class B services *before* applying the 80% co-insurance.
- If the deductible IS met, apply the 80% co-insurance directly to the billed Class B amount.
- Class A services are always paid at 100%, regardless of deductible status.
